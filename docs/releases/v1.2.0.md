## New
- **Messages last 24 hours**, in every chat, even when the server restarts. They used to all disappear whenever the server went offline. A message sent to someone who was away still reaches them after a restart.
- **Remove friends** from your friends list (the **Remove** button next to **Message**), and **close conversations** with **Close chat** at the top of a DM, or the **×** next to it in the sidebar.
- **Don't list me**: turn it on in **Settings → Privacy** and you won't show up under "Everyone in this network". People can still add you by typing your exact username.
- **@everyone** from the developer, in General and Announcements, pings the whole network.
