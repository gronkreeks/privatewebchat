## New
- **Replies**: hover over a message and click **Reply**. Your message shows the one you replied to, and clicking the quote jumps back to it. If someone replies to you in General, you get a notification.
- **ASCII GIFs**: click **GIF** next to Send and pick one: a cat, a rocket, a dancing stick figure and more. They play for everyone in the chat.
- **No more failed messages when the server is asleep**: anything you send says **Connecting…**, privatewebchat wakes the server up, and your message goes out as soon as it's back.
- **Automatic updates**: from now on, when privatewebchat opens and there's a new version, it installs it by itself and shows you what's new.

## Updating from 1.1.4 or earlier
Just this once, download privatewebchat from this page and install it the way you did before. After that it updates itself.
